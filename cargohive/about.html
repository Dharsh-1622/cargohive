About CargoHive

We built CargoHive so small businesses can access the cubic metres they need — and providers can put leftover capacity to work.

Why We Exist

Capacity Is Valuable. Empty Space Shouldn't Travel Alone.

Regional freight corridors move constantly, yet a surprising amount of cubic capacity rides unused. At the same time, exporters and traders with smaller consignments struggle to find a trip that fits a partial load without overpaying for a full vehicle.

CargoHive was created to close that gap — making available space discoverable, bookable, and validated before anyone commits.

Vision

Make transportation capacity easier to discover and use.

We imagine a market where leftover CBM is as visible as a seat on a bus — searchable by route, date, and cargo needs.

Mission

Help small businesses move cargo smarter.

Give traders access to suitable logistics capacity while helping providers improve utilisation on trips they already run.

Our Approach

Platform Ecosystem

Three roles, one shared capacity workflow.

📦

Trader

Search routes, enter cargo details, validate fit, book CBM, and follow shipment status.

🚛

Provider

Publish vehicles and free capacity, accept bookings, and manage trip utilisation.

🛡️

Admin

Verify logistics partners and oversee users, vehicles, and bookings across the platform.

Differentiation
