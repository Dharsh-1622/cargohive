Concept

What Is Shared Cargo Space?

CBM (cubic metres) measures volume. If a truck has 10 CBM total and 5 CBM is already taken, 5 CBM remains open for another shipment that fits.

Example Trip
Vehicle: 10 CBM · Occupied: 5 CBM · Available: 5 CBM · Trader needs: 2 CBM
