What does CBM mean?

CBM (Cubic Meter) measures the physical volume of your cargo. CargoHive uses CBM to help traders book only the space they need, rather than paying for an entire empty container.

Length (m) × Width (m) × Height (m) = CBM Example: 2m × 1m × 1m = 2 CBM
